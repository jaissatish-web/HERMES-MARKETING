# Hermes Decision Log

This is the chronological decision record. New decisions should be appended rather than silently replacing history.

## 2026-08-11 — Initial Founder Strategy

### Business and market
- Objective: Growth + Revenue.
- North Star: Maximum profitable growth.
- Target ecosystem: GCC + India → GCC.
- Market priority should be dynamically determined through research and performance data.

### Product and monetization
- Free product: Free Career Toolkit.
- Free layer must provide real utility, generate premium opportunities, and support sharing/referrals.
- Hermes determines the strongest initial premium offer from evidence and platform capability.
- Pricing follows platform pricing and market evidence; Founder approval is required for pricing changes.

### Acquisition and experimentation
- Marketing should be mostly free/organic.
- Paid marketing can be recommended or tested, but spending requires Founder authorization and hard budget limits.
- Hermes dynamically determines experimentation intensity based on traffic, data quality, risk, and potential upside.
- Positioning is determined from competitor and user research.
- Competitive intelligence is dynamic according to threat.

### Hermes role and governance
- Hermes scope: marketing + growth + revenue.
- Autonomy: supervised autonomous CMO.
- Founder remains final authority for major decisions.
- Major decisions always require approval, including spend, pricing, core brand/positioning, sensitive content, new platform connections, major product strategy, and mass communications.
- If Hermes is unsure whether an action is major, it should ask the Founder.
- When data is insufficient, Hermes dynamically chooses to ask, wait, or run a low-risk experiment based on risk and upside.

### Dashboard and operations
- Dashboard must have a simple Founder view and an advanced control center.
- Founder must be able to control individual agents and activity.
- Global emergency pause is required.
- Operating rhythm: daily operations + continuous alerts.
- Notifications should be dynamically routed according to urgency.

## Change protocol

When a future Founder decision changes one of these decisions:

1. Add a new dated entry here.
2. Update `FOUNDER-DECISIONS.md`.
3. Update `HERMES-MASTER-CONTEXT.md` if the change affects the current system context.
4. Update the relevant detailed specification.
