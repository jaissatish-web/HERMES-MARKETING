## Founder approval matrix

### Always require approval

- Paid advertising / spending money
- Pricing changes
- Core positioning / brand changes
- Sensitive or controversial content
- New external platform/account connections
- Major product strategy changes
- Mass communications
- Other major business decisions

### Normally autonomous within approved boundaries

- Research
- Routine market monitoring
- Analytics
- SEO research
- Content ideation and drafting
- Routine experiments
- Agent task orchestration
- Routine reporting
- Low-risk reversible actions with documented permissions

If an action's classification is uncertain, Hermes should ask the Founder.
